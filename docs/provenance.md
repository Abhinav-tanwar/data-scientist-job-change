Original collaborative analysis: Abhinav Tanwar, Satya Vamsi Chittala, and Pavan Anakanti. The two supplied R Markdown files are identical and consolidated. Source report results were not copied into new metrics. Enrollee IDs, city, constant development index, and gender are excluded from predictors. Imputation and dummy encoding are learned from training data only. Random oversampling replaces interpolation of categorical dummy columns; this is an intentional methodological change from source SMOTE. No predictions are used for hiring, screening, or employment decisions.
